Ready For The Holidays? 3 Things To Fix Before Customers Start Searching

November 14, 2026 | 5 min read | Derrick D. Patton
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Holiday Fixes

You already did the hard part. Somebody heard about you from a friend, saw your truck, or remembered you from last year. Now they're pulling out their phone, searching your name, and they're ready to call or book.

But if your hours are wrong, your number doesn't work, or they can't figure out how to reach you, they won't wait around. They'll tap on the next business in the list.

Here are the three things to check this week, before the holiday rush hits.

Fix 1: Your Hours

Pull up your business on Google, then on Facebook, then on your own website. Do all three say the same thing?

More often than not, they don't. Maybe you changed your hours last spring and only updated Facebook. Maybe your website still says you're open Saturdays when you stopped doing that a year ago. A customer doesn't know which one is right. They just know one of them was wrong, and they drove over or called for nothing.

The holidays make this worse. Think about:

  • Are you closing early on Christmas Eve or New Year's Eve?
  • Are you closed Thanksgiving Day, or the day after?
  • Are you taking a few days off between Christmas and New Year's?
  • Are you open extra hours for the season?

Here's the fix: Pick one place to be your source of truth. For most small businesses, that's your Google Business Profile, because that's where most people see your hours first. Get it exactly right, holiday hours included. Then go match Facebook and your website to it. Any time something changes, update Google first, then the other two.

Fix 2: Your Phone Number

This one takes 30 seconds. Grab your cell phone, search for your business, and call the number that shows up.

Does it ring to you? Or does it go to an old line, a disconnected number, or a voicemail box that's been full since July?

While you're at it, look at your website on your phone. Can you tap the number to call it, or is it just plain text that someone has to copy, switch apps, and paste? Most of your customers are searching from their phone. Every extra step is a chance for them to give up.

Check the number everywhere it shows up: Google, Facebook, your website, NextDoor, Yelp, and any directory you've ever signed up for. An old number on any one of them is a lost customer you'll never even hear about.

Here's the fix: Make sure the same, working number is on every listing, and make sure it's tap-to-call on your website. If you can't answer during the busy season, make sure your voicemail greeting is current and tells people when they'll hear back.

Fix 3: How To Book Or Reach You

Some people don't want to call. They want to book online, send a message, or fill out a quick form asking for a quote. So ask yourself: can someone reach you in one tap?

That could be:

  • A booking link
  • A contact or quote request form
  • Facebook Messenger
  • A NextDoor message
  • A text number

It doesn't matter which one you use. What matters is that it's the one you actually answer. A contact form that sends to an email inbox you never check is worse than no form at all, because the customer thinks they reached you.

If someone has to hunt through three pages of your website to find out how to get in touch, they won't. They'll move on.

Here's the fix: Pick the one or two ways you really respond to, and put that same link front and center everywhere: the top of your website, your Google profile, your Facebook page, and your social bios. Then test it yourself. Book a fake appointment or send yourself a message and make sure it lands where you'll see it.

Quick Checklist Before The Holidays

  • Hours match on Google, Facebook, and your website, holiday hours included
  • Phone number rings to you and is tap-to-call on your website
  • Booking or contact link works and goes to a place you actually check

You Don't Need A Whole New Website
