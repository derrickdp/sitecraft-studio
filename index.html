What We Do

I’m Derrick D. Patton, a software development engineer and manager with over 26 years of experience designing, building, and leading software solutions for organizations of all sizes. After decades in enterprise technology, I founded SiteCraft Studio to bring the same reliability, structure, and technical excellence to small businesses, nonprofits, and independent professionals—serving small businesses in Chicago and the greater Chicagoland area.

Throughout my career, I’ve led engineering teams, delivered complex systems, and developed a deep understanding of what makes technology both stable and user-friendly. Today, my mission is to help entrepreneurs and organizations build modern, professional websites without the cost or complexity typically associated with enterprise-level solutions.

When you work with SiteCraft Studio, you’re partnering with someone who understands code, design, and business strategy. I take pride in getting every detail right so your website doesn’t just look great—it performs effectively for your business.

Why online presence matters

  • Most people now search online before choosing a local business, so if you are not visible, you simply do not get considered.
  • A strong online presence lets you reach far beyond your physical location and stay in front of the right audience every day, not just when they walk past your door.
  • Consistent, professional visibility across search, social media, and reviews builds trust, loyalty, and long-term brand awareness.

How your website helps

  • Your website acts like a 24/7 salesperson that answers questions, builds trust, and can even take orders while you sleep.
  • A well-designed site boosts credibility; research shows most consumers judge a company’s trustworthiness by its website design alone.
  • With the right content and SEO, your site helps you get found on Google when people search for the products or services you offer, driving more qualified leads to your business.
